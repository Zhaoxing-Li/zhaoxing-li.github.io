Sim-GAIL: Student Behaviour Simulator

GAIL-based student behaviour simulator learning decision-making policies from expert trajectories. Demonstrated improved policy fidelity and generalisation to unseen learners, outperforming baseline Deep Q-Learning and supervised sequence models across benchmark educational datasets.

LBKT: LSTM-BERT Knowledge Tracing

Hybrid deep learning architecture combining a BERT encoder with an LSTM sequential layer to capture long-range dependencies in student learning histories. Integrated Rasch-based embeddings for question difficulty and cognitive discrimination, achieving state-of-the-art AUC on benchmark educational datasets.
