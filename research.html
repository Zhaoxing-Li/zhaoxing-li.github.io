RESEARCH AREAS

🛡️

AI Safety & Governance

My work addresses how to design AI systems that behave reliably, transparently, and in alignment with human values. This includes developing technical frameworks for LLM safety, mitigating prompt-injection attacks, building structured dialogue agents that resist manipulation, and proposing the Citizen-Centric MAS (C-LLMAS) paradigm for accountable participatory AI with ethical safeguards by design.

🤖

Multi-Agent Systems

I design and evaluate architectures for heterogeneous multi-agent environments — particularly those involving LLM-based agents operating in safety-critical or socially sensitive contexts. This includes Human-in-the-Loop multi-robot collaboration (HMCF), runtime safety orchestrators that prevent unsafe behaviours, and agent frameworks for online group consensus building (PTFA).

MASLLM AgentsHuman-in-the-LoopRobotics
🎓

Educational AI

I develop intelligent systems that personalise learning at scale. This spans knowledge tracing models (LBKT, MLFBK) that capture student cognitive states using BERT and LSTM architectures, student behaviour simulators (SimStu, Sim-GAIL) using Decision Transformers and GAIL, and RAG-augmented tutoring systems (TutorLLM) that deliver context-aware, student-state-specific recommendations.

💻

Human-Computer Interaction

My HCI work studies how people interact with AI-mediated systems and how design choices shape those interactions. Topics include AI-mediated text communication, gesture-based interfaces for ASL learning in VR/AR environments, personalised AI assistants, and user modelling in intelligent systems. I prioritise empirical user studies with real participants.
