Who I am

ABOUT

Research Fellow · University of Southampton · AI Safety & Multi-Agent Systems

I am a Research Fellow in the School of Electronics and Computer Science at the University of Southampton, holding a PhD in Computer Science from Durham University.

My research focuses on AI risk, technical governance, and safety-aware multi-agent systems. I address key challenges in the responsible deployment of advanced AI, including LLM safety, human oversight, prompt-injection mitigation, and the design of accountable, citizen-centric AI architectures that embed safeguards by design.

I am currently a Research Fellow on the EPSRC-funded Citizen-Centric AI Systems (CCAIS) project and a Co-Investigator on the EPSRC-funded For the FATES of Africa project, developing governance-oriented frameworks for trustworthy multi-agent systems and studying the societal impacts of AI in resource-constrained contexts.

I have published 40 peer-reviewed papers, with 335+ citations and an h-index of 12, and bring additional entrepreneurial experience translating research into real-world impact.

"Designing AI systems that are safe, accountable, and genuinely citizen-centric — from architecture to governance."
